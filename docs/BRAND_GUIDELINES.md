# DayCrunch — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Name:** DAYCRUNCH  
**Tagline:** Crunch Better. Live Better.  
**Domain:** daycrunch.in

## Brand Personality

Fresh · Healthy · Energetic · Premium · Youthful · Lifestyle-oriented · Gift-worthy

## Logo

- **DAY** in Deep Plum `#5B2A86`
- **CRUNCH** in Sunset Orange `#FF6B35`
- Leaf/nut symbol in Pistachio Green `#7BC043`
- Minimum clear space: height of the "D" on all sides
- Works on light backgrounds (primary) and dark backgrounds (white variant)

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary | Sunset Orange | `#FF6B35` |
| Secondary | Deep Plum | `#5B2A86` |
| Accent | Pistachio Green | `#7BC043` |
| Highlight | Golden Yellow | `#FFC857` |
| Background | Cream White | `#FFF8F0` |
| Secondary BG | Soft Beige | `#F8F4EE` |
| Text | Dark Charcoal | `#1F2937` |
| Muted | Gray | `#6B7280` |

## Typography

- **Primary Font:** Outfit (Google Fonts)
- **Headings:** Black (900) weight
- **Body:** Regular (400) / Medium (500)
- **CTAs:** Semibold (600) / Bold (700)

## Voice & Tone

- Confident but approachable
- Health-focused without being preachy
- Premium but not pretentious
- Energetic and youthful
- Indian context, global quality standards

## Photography Style

- Bright, natural lighting
- Lifestyle contexts (office, gym, home, gifting)
- Vibrant product close-ups showing texture
- Warm color grading aligned with brand palette

## Do's

- Use rounded corners (2xl/3xl radius)
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Show real product texture and quality

## Don'ts

- Avoid traditional "dry fruit store" aesthetics
- No cluttered layouts or heavy borders
- Don't use colors outside the palette
- Avoid stock photos that feel generic
